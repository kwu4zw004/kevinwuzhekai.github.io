Case study 01 / Dahua / HuaRay · 2023

AMR Lift Reliability

Investigating structural failures, revising a lifting mechanism, and validating the redesign through full-load durability testing.

Engineering focus

Root-cause analysis · Abaqus · Durability testing

My role

I led the failure investigation and mechanical redesign, designed the durability-test setup, and documented validation results.

Engineering approach

I used full-system Abaqus FEA to investigate load distribution. The redesign addressed drive layout, structural interfaces, and material selection. Physical testing used a laser cycle counter, noise measurements, and reference marks to monitor cracking and displacement.

Validation and outcome

The revised mechanism completed 100,000 full-load lifting cycles without structural failure and was adopted for production. I documented the test findings in a validation report.
