Case study 02 / Waterbury Button Company · 2024–2025

Button Assembly Automation

From a repetitive manual operation to a working pneumatic assembly machine: requirements, mechanism design, fabrication, and commissioning.

Engineering focus

Mechanism design · Pneumatics · Delivery

My role

I led the mechanical architecture and SolidWorks design within the project team, covering component selection, fabrication, documentation, and commissioning.

Engineering approach

I translated the process into stages for feeding, alignment, and assembly. Pneumatic actuation, dedicated die fixtures, and feeding hardware supported repeatable assembly. I maintained the bill of materials and supported procurement and prototype iteration.

Validation and outcome

The team fabricated and commissioned a working assembly machine at the customer facility. The system improved the original process while remaining below the project budget.
