Case study 03 / Hikrobot · 2026

Industrial Robot Development

Developing mechanical components and supporting their transition from CAD and prototype assembly to validation and production release.

Engineering focus

GD&T · Component selection · Integration

My role

I developed components and assemblies and owned an AMR idler-wheel module through design, fabrication, assembly, validation, and production release.

Engineering approach

I designed more than 20 mechanical parts with drawings and GD&T. I supported motor, gearbox, and bearing selection using load calculations, structural analysis, and fit checks. I designed sensor mounts, enclosures, and cable-retention features and maintained BOM and engineering-change submissions for senior approval.

Validation and delivery

For TP6 work, I executed durability testing against criteria defined by a senior mechanical engineer, with test procedures, validation reports, and issue logs. I also coordinated three technicians and helped resolve more than 20 supplier and assembly issues, completing a prototype build in two weeks against a three-week plan.
